1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

Khorchapati-খরচাপাতি is a privacy-first personal expense, budget, savings goal, and loan tracker. Your privacy is fundamental to our design: we do not operate any backend servers that collect, store, or process your personal or financial data.

2. Data Storage

  • 100% On-Device Storage: All expenses, items, budgets, goals, loans, categories, and recurring payment records you enter are stored exclusively on your own device using browser IndexedDB storage.
  • On-Device Smart Features: Smart item parsing and category classification run locally on your device using on-device NLP and WebAssembly/WebGPU models. Your financial notes are never sent to external servers for analysis.
  • Backups & Exports: You may optionally export or import your data as a local file on your device. You maintain complete control over these backup files.

3. Data Collection & Third-Party Sharing

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not require account registration, sign-in, email addresses, phone numbers, or identity verification.
  • No Financial Account Access: The app does not connect to banks, payment cards, or mobile financial services.
  • No Third-Party Sharing or Selling: Because we never collect your data, we never sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Deletion

Because all data resides locally on your device, you can permanently delete your data at any time by using the in-app Reset/Clear Data option in Settings, clearing the app's storage in your Android system settings, or uninstalling the application.
